Privacy Policy

Ceylon Rails
Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ceylon Rails app handles, where it is stored, and when it is sent to another service.

Information stored on your device

Ceylon Rails stores your saved trains, recent station choices, preferred route, and display settings on your device using the platform's local preferences storage. You can remove saved trains in the app's Settings. Removing the app also removes its local preferences according to your device's operating-system behavior.

Information sent to RDMNS

Live timetable and train-detail information is provided by the RDMNS service at radar.hesn.xyz. When you use these features, Ceylon Rails connects to the service to retrieve the requested train information.

The app does not request access to your device's precise location. Its train positions come from reports supplied by the RDMNS service, not from GPS data collected by Ceylon Rails.

RDMNS is a separate service. Its handling of information is governed by its own policies and practices, which are outside Dan Solutions' control.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Ceylon Rails does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not sell your personal information.

Data retention and choices

Your saved trains and preferences remain in local app storage until you remove them or remove the app. You can stop using the live features at any time. Information handled by RDMNS is subject to that service's own practices.

Children's privacy

Ceylon Rails is a general-purpose travel information app and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above indicates when it was last revised.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, contact Dan Solutions through the support page.
