Privacy Policy for Oshani Traders

Last Updated: 28/12/2025

Introduction

Oshani Traders ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Oshani Traders (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

1. Contact Information

When you contact us through the in-app contact form or submit a pre-order request, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • WhatsApp number (optional, for pre-orders)
  • Message content (for contact form)
  • Vehicle information (for pre-order requests)

2. Notification Preferences

To provide you with vehicle updates and notifications, we collect:

  • Notification topic subscriptions (e.g., preOrder, preOwn, unregistered)
  • Vehicle-specific subscriptions (brand, model, year combinations)
  • Notification preferences and settings

3. Device and Usage Information

We automatically collect certain information about your device and how you use the App:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and build number
  • Unique device identifier (UUID) for notification management
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications
  • Usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics, if enabled)
  • Crash reports and error logs (via Firebase Crashlytics, if enabled)

4. Local Storage

We store certain information locally on your device:

  • User ID (unique identifier for notification management)
  • Contact information (name, email, mobile, WhatsApp) - saved after form submissions
  • Notification topic subscriptions
  • Vehicle-specific subscriptions
  • App preferences and settings

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide vehicle information: Display vehicle listings, details, and availability
  • Send notifications: Deliver push notifications about vehicle updates, new arrivals, price changes, and vehicle availability
  • Process inquiries: Respond to your contact form submissions and pre-order requests
  • Improve the App: Analyze usage patterns, identify bugs, and enhance user experience
  • Communicate with you: Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Ensure functionality: Manage notification subscriptions and app preferences

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect and process your information:

Firebase Services (Google)

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Handles push notifications and manages notification subscriptions
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects usage analytics and app performance data (if enabled)
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports and error logs (if enabled)
  • Firebase Remote Config: Manages app configuration and feature flags

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Email Service

  • Purpose: Processes contact form submissions and pre-order requests
  • Data Collected: Name, email address, mobile number, WhatsApp number (if provided), message content, and vehicle information
  • Data Processing: Your contact information is sent via email to our support team for processing your inquiries

Data Storage and Security

Cloud Storage

Your notification preferences and device tokens are stored securely using:

  • Standard TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging infrastructure

Local Storage

Information is stored locally on your device using:

  • SharedPreferences (Android) / UserDefaults (iOS)
  • Encrypted storage mechanisms provided by the operating system

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Secure data transmission
  • Encrypted local storage
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We may share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Firebase (Google) for notification delivery and analytics
  • Email service providers for processing contact form submissions

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access and Update

  • You can access and update your contact information through the App
  • Contact information is automatically saved after form submissions for convenience

Manage Notifications

  • You can manage notification preferences in the App settings
  • You can subscribe or unsubscribe from vehicle categories (preOrder, preOwn, unregistered)
  • You can subscribe or unsubscribe from specific vehicle notifications
  • You can disable push notifications through your device settings

Clear Local Data

  • You can clear locally stored data by uninstalling the App
  • Note: Clearing app data will remove your notification preferences and saved contact information

Opt-Out of Analytics

  • You can opt-out of Firebase Analytics by disabling analytics in your device settings
  • Note: Some analytics may still be collected for essential app functionality

Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Contact Information: Stored locally on your device until you uninstall the App or clear app data
  • Notification Preferences: Stored locally and in Firebase until you unsubscribe or uninstall the App
  • FCM Tokens: Stored in Firebase for notification delivery until you uninstall the App or disable notifications
  • Analytics Data: Retained in accordance with Firebase's data retention policies

If you uninstall the App:

  • Locally stored data will be deleted
  • FCM tokens will be removed from our notification system
  • Some anonymized analytics data may be retained for statistical purposes

Children's Privacy

Oshani Traders is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries, including:

  • Firebase services (Google Cloud Platform) - data may be processed in various global locations
  • Email service providers - data may be processed in various global locations

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App (if applicable)
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Posting updates on our website (if applicable)

Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: danusha.99h@gmail.com

In-App: Use the "Contact Us" feature in the App settings

Additional Information

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including:

  • The right to know what personal information we collect
  • The right to delete your personal information
  • The right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your information)

European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to rectification
  • The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to object to processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided above.
