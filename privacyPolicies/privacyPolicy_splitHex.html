Privacy Policy for Split Hex

Last Updated: 22/10/2026

Introduction

Split Hex ("we," "our," or "us") helps you share expenses with other people and plan your own budget. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Split Hex mobile application (the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Information We Collect

1. Account Information

When you sign in with Apple, Google or your phone number, we collect:

  • Your name, and the email address or phone number you sign in with
  • Your @userID, which you choose and which others use to find you
  • Other email addresses and a WhatsApp number, only if you link them in Settings
  • A profile picture: one you upload, the picture from your Google account, or your WhatsApp profile picture if you linked a WhatsApp number
  • Your home currency and when your account was created

2. Expenses, Groups and Payments

To split costs and show balances, we store what you and the people you share with enter:

  • Groups, their members and settings
  • Expenses: description, amount, currency, date, category, who paid and how it was split, notes
  • Receipt photos you attach
  • Payments recorded between people, and the activity history of each group
  • Friends and friend requests

When you add someone to a group or an expense by email or phone number before they join, we store that email address or phone number so their share is waiting for them when they sign up.

3. Budget Information

  • Budget cycles, income, savings and investment amounts you enter
  • Spending goals per category and recurring expenses
  • Your choice of motivation messages

4. Device Information

  • A push notification token, so we can tell you when something changes in your groups
  • Basic technical information needed to keep the App working, such as the App version

Your theme, tutorial progress and a daily count of expenses added are kept only on your device.

5. Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads from Unity Ads. Unity may collect information about your device and your interaction with ads (for example, device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and which ads were shown or tapped) to show and measure ads. The App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it does not give your advertising identifier to anyone. See Unity's privacy policy.

6. Purchases

Split Hex Premium is bought through Apple. We do not see or store your payment details; Apple tells the App only whether your subscription is active.

7. Automatic Expenses from Bank Messages (optional)

Automatic expenses is off until you turn it on in Settings > Automatic expenses. When it is on, the App looks at new text messages on your phone to find bank transactions and adds them for you:

  • Android: the App asks for permission to receive text messages. It sees only messages that arrive while the feature is on, and it cannot read the messages already on your phone.
  • iPhone: a Shortcuts automation that you set up passes each new message to the App.

Each message is read on your phone. From a card payment, or from money coming in if you choose to add it, the App keeps only the amount, the currency, the shop or sender's name and the date, and sends only those to our server, where they become a personal expense or an income record in your account. The text of your messages is never stored or sent. Messages that are not bank transactions, such as conversations and verification codes, are ignored. If you limit the feature to some of your cards, the 4 digits you enter for each card stay on your phone.

A short list of the transactions added today, and the reason and time of the last message that was skipped, are kept only on your phone. You can turn the feature off at any time in Settings > Automatic expenses (on Android you can also remove the permission in your phone's settings). Expenses and income already added stay until you delete them.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To calculate who owes whom, show balances, and keep each group's history
  • To let people you share expenses with see the expenses, payments and balances they are part of
  • To let others find you by @userID, email address or phone number when they add a friend
  • To track your budget and show charts and reminders
  • To add expenses and income from your bank's text messages, if you turn on Automatic expenses
  • To send notifications and verification codes
  • To show ads in the free version, and to remove them for Premium subscribers

We do not sell your personal information.

Who Can See Your Information

Members of a group can see that group's expenses, payments, members and balances, including names and profile pictures. A friend can see the balance between the two of you and the expenses you share. Until someone accepts your friend request, they see none of the groups or expenses you share with them.

Third-Party Services

We use these services to run the App. Each handles data only to provide its service:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and in-app purchases
  • Google Firebase: sign-in (Google and phone number), push notifications and app settings
  • Cloudflare: hosting of our server, database and stored pictures, and sending verification emails
  • Brevo: sending verification emails
  • Green-API: looking up the WhatsApp profile picture for a WhatsApp number you link
  • Unity Ads: ads in the free version

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is limited to the App's server. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: you can see and change your name, picture, linked emails, WhatsApp number and home currency in Settings.
  • Export: you can export a group's expenses as a CSV file.
  • Delete your account: Settings > Delete my account. Your balances must be settled first, so no one is left owing money to an account that no longer exists.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Automatic expenses: off unless you turn it on; you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Automatic expenses.
  • Ads: Split Hex Premium removes all ads.

Data Retention

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in details, linked emails and WhatsApp number, profile pictures, devices, friends and budget, and your Firebase sign-in. Expenses that involved only you are marked as deleted and no longer shown. Expenses and payments you shared with other people stay in their groups so their balances remain correct, shown under "Deleted user" instead of your name. Your @userID is retired so that no one else can take it.

Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your Split Hex account at any time:

  • In the app: open Settings, then Delete my account. Balances must be settled first.
  • Without the app: email Danusha.99h@gmail.com from the email address on your account (or include your @userID), with the subject "Delete my Split Hex account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept is described under Data Retention above. To delete only some of your data, such as an expense, a receipt photo, your profile picture or a linked email, remove it in the app, or ask us by email.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own. By using the App, you agree to this transfer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Your continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us
